# Glove model

The user-supplied `glove-hand-right.fbx`, and its mirrored/skinned mesh derivatives
in `left_hand.tscn` and `right_hand.tscn`, are based on **Glove by Quaternius**.

- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/l1zv4LaA4I

- Source listing date: August 26, 2021

# Independent test skeletons

`tools/hand_rigs/` retains the skeleton-only fixtures extracted from the
[Godot OpenXR hand tracking demo](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects/tree/6ad6167e0577fe3622c18546138f456b107ce93c/xr/openxr_hand_tracking_demo/assets/gltf),
revision `6ad6167e0577fe3622c18546138f456b107ce93c`. They are used only to test
tracking with a different hand anatomy. Their MIT notice follows.
